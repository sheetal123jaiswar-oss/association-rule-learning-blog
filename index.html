01 / GET THE IDEA

What is Association Rule Learning?

Association Rule Learning is a data-mining technique used to discover relationships between items in large datasets. It is often used in Market Basket Analysis, where each transaction contains products purchased together.

The antecedent is the left-hand side of the rule; the consequent is the right-hand side. This rule describes an observed relationship, not proof that bread causes butter purchases.

03 / THREE IMPORTANT MEASURES

Support, confidence and lift

📊 Support

Support(A → B) = count(A and B) / total transactions

How often do A and B appear together in the whole dataset?

🎯 Confidence

Confidence(A → B) = count(A and B) / count(A)

Among transactions containing A, what fraction also contain B?

🚀 Lift

Lift(A → B) = Confidence(A → B) / Support(B)

How much more often do A and B occur together than expected if they were independent?

Worked example: Bread → Butter

From the five sample transactions above, bread appears in T1, T2, T3 and T5 (4 transactions). Butter appears in T1, T2, T4 and T5 (4 transactions). Both appear together in T1, T2 and T5 (3 transactions).

Support = 3 / 5 = 0.60 = 60%
Confidence = 3 / 4 = 0.75 = 75%
Lift = 0.75 / (4 / 5) = 0.9375

Interpretation: 60% of all transactions contain both items. Of transactions containing bread, 75% also contain butter. The lift is below 1 in this sample, so the pair occurs slightly less often than expected under independence.

04 / THE ALGORITHM

How does Apriori work?

Apriori finds frequent itemsets using a key idea: if an itemset is frequent, all of its non-empty subsets must also be frequent. If a subset is infrequent, a larger set containing it cannot be frequent.

Step 1 — Set minimum support
Choose a minimum frequency threshold for item combinations.
Step 2 — Count individual items
Calculate support for each item and remove those below the threshold.
Step 3 — Create larger candidates
Combine frequent itemsets to form candidate pairs, triples and so on.
Step 4 — Prune candidates
Remove candidates with any infrequent subset.
Step 5 — Repeat
Count candidates and continue until no larger frequent itemsets remain.
Step 6 — Generate rules
Calculate confidence and optionally lift, then keep rules that meet chosen criteria.
Minimum support and confidence are user-selected thresholds. Their appropriate values depend on the dataset and the business question.

07 / COMPARE & EVALUATE

Advantages and limitations

✅ Advantages

  • Finds understandable co-occurrence patterns.
  • Can process large transaction collections with suitable methods.
  • Supports recommendation and product-planning ideas.
  • Does not require labelled target classes.

⚠️ Limitations

  • May generate many rules.
  • Rare but useful patterns can be missed by high support thresholds.
  • Patterns do not prove causation.
  • Apriori can be slow on dense data with many candidates.

💡 Best practice

Consider support, confidence, lift, business usefulness and validation together. A high confidence rule can be misleading when its consequent is already very common.

09 / THE FINAL TAKEAWAY

Small patterns. Smarter decisions. 🌈

Association Rule Learning is a useful data-mining technique for discovering items or events that frequently occur together. In Market Basket Analysis, it helps explore customer purchasing patterns and can inform recommendations, promotions and product placement.

Support measures how common a pattern is, confidence measures the conditional frequency of a rule, and lift compares that relationship with what would be expected under independence. Apriori uses frequent-itemset properties to reduce unnecessary candidate exploration.

However, rules show association rather than causation. Thresholds, data quality, validation and real-world usefulness should all be considered before acting on a discovered pattern.

⭐ Remember

A useful rule is not just frequent — it should also be meaningful, reliable and relevant to the problem.
